# 自然完整成人刀剑中性姿态：首图

Use case: stylized-concept.
Reference image: the supplied approved swordsman.png is the sole figure/style reference. Use this complete painted adult as the reference for coherent anatomy, facial structure, costume, weapon and hand-painted finish. Do not use any previous cutout atlas, separated limbs, rig render, collar patch or failed composite as an anatomical template.

Create ONE new, complete full-body painting of the SAME adult swordsman in a natural neutral ready stance. This is a whole figure, never an exploded parts sheet. The purpose is to establish correct complete human anatomy, real sword grip and continuous garment folds before any layering work. Every limb, wrist, elbow, shoulder and garment is painted continuously as part of one complete figure.

Identity and style: preserve the reference's adult facial structure, calm focused expression, dark tied topknot with plain light cloth tie, restrained short facial hair, medium athletic build, approximately 7 to 7.5 adult head proportions, warm skin, and three-quarter view facing screen right. Fine hand-painted semi-realism with controlled dark ink contour, clear painterly shading, subtle visible brushwork and fabric texture. Do not turn the face into a blank mannequin or invent a new ornate hero. Match the same muted olive linen crossed-collar tunic, cream inner collar and rolled cuffs, brown cloth waist sash, loose cream trousers, dark calf wraps and worn brown cloth shoes. Ordinary late-Han travel clothing; no armor, shoulder pads, badges, jewelry, additional collar, detached shoulder patch or costume redesign.

Pose: balanced, almost upright neutral combat readiness, shoulders relaxed, torso stable, hips and knees anatomically aligned. Feet about shoulder-width apart with one slightly forward, both complete soles at believable contact level; only a slight knee bend, not a deep squat, crossed legs or a wide lunge. The near/right arm hangs naturally with a mild elbow bend. The right hand holds the SAME simple slightly curved single-edged Chinese sword/saber from the reference by its dark wrapped grip, at the side of the right hip/thigh. Wrist straight and naturally aligned with forearm; thumb and fingers convincingly wrap the actual grip behind the small plain bronze guard, pommel behind the hand, blade in front. The blade points diagonally down and forward to screen right, clear of the body and feet. Show exactly one weapon. The far/left hand is open and relaxed in a modest ready gesture near the lower abdomen, fingers naturally arranged, not a clenched punch and not gripping a second weapon.

Continuity: shoulder-to-upper-arm, elbow-to-forearm, forearm-to-wrist and wrist-to-hand must be anatomically continuous. Clothing seams and folds follow the complete arm and torso load; no circular cut caps, tubular sockets, overlapping duplicate cuffs, separate stuffed shoulder pieces, floating hand, floating sword or pasted collar. The head meets a natural neck and one coherent crossed collar. The belt binds the same tunic continuously around the body. Both legs have believable knee directions, continuous wraps and full attached feet.

Composition: one full figure plus held weapon on a square transparent canvas, roughly 1536x1536 suggested. Keep the whole topknot, both hands, all fingers, both full shoes, guard, pommel and sword tip inside the frame with generous safety margin. Leave enough space to the right for the blade; do not crop any extremity. No scenery, floor, cast shadow, title, labels, grid, UI, border or extra pose. Real transparent alpha outside the figure, including negative spaces between arm/body/weapon and between legs; no white matte or painted checkerboard.

This is a neutral pose source painting only, not animation, a new actor profile or an equipment change. No gore, injury or battle effects. Do not decompose or cut the character into parts. The first image will be inspected before any second pose is produced.
